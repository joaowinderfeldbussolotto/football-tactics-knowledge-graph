## 2. Errors by arm

Hallucination: a wrong answer given as an answer (not an abstention, not a format error). On unanswerable questions, any answer other than `no_data` is a hallucination. Abstention: `no_data` on a question that has an answer. Format error: no valid `Answer` after the output retries (or the tool/request limit was hit).
